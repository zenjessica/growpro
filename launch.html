WHY WE WILL NEVER PROMISE YOU INCOME
Federal law, the FTC's Business Opportunity Rule, prohibits unsubstantiated income or earnings claims when offering a business opportunity. Companies are fined for every violation. Read the rule at FTC.gov →
So we do not do fantasy math, and you should sprint from anyone in this space who does. What we can do is show you the publicly reported market data, the demand signals advertisers pay real money against, and let you do your own math. Your results depend on your offer, your effort, and your market.
GLP-1 & WEIGHT LOSS
$100B+ by 2030
Major bank research desks (Goldman Sachs, J.P. Morgan, Morgan Stanley) have publicly projected the global anti-obesity and GLP-1 market at roughly $100 billion or more by 2030. Demand proof: agency benchmarks put GLP-1 and telehealth search clicks at $8 to $25+ each. Advertisers do not pay that for imaginary demand.
HORMONE THERAPY
A reopened category
Public market research houses estimate the menopause and HRT market in the tens of billions globally and growing. After two decades suppressed by a since-lifted warning, professional bodies including NAMS, ACOG, and the Endocrine Society now publicly support HRT as first-line treatment for many patients. Roughly 6,000 US women enter menopause every day, published US census-based estimate.
PEPTIDES
$40B+ and reclassifying
Peptide therapeutics are publicly estimated as a $40B+ global market with projections to roughly double within the decade. The near-term catalyst is regulatory: 12 peptides are moving through the FDA's PCAC review process between 2026 and 2027. First to market wins categories.
TELEHEALTH, THE MACRO
Hundreds of billions
Public research firms project global telehealth in the hundreds of billions of dollars by the early 2030s. Care moved home after 2020 and it is not moving back. The infrastructure to serve hormones, peptides, and labs under one brand is the whitespace.
"WHAT IS YOUR CLIENTS' CPA?" (THE HONEST ANSWER)
Our clients' acquisition costs are confidential, exactly as yours will be when you are the client. What we can share is public benchmark data: GLP-1 and telehealth keywords run $8 to $25+ per click, while hormone-symptom searches (brain fog, night sweats, fatigue) have historically run a fraction of that. Your cost per acquisition will be a function of your offer, your creative, and your market, which is precisely what gets built here.
