Marketing Is Three Different Jobs
Organic socialTrust
People look you up before they ever buy. This is your business card and your digital footprint. It does not buy traffic. It converts the traffic you already earned.
Paid adsTraffic
The faucet. On today, off today. The only lever that creates demand on command. Two separate numbers: our management fee, and the ad spend you pay the platform directly.
Blogs, Shorts, emailCompounding
Assets that keep working after you publish them. Slowest to start, cheapest to run. This is a month six play, not a week one play.
The order matters more than the budget. Founders get burned buying the compounding layer before the faucet is on, which means paying for reach they have not created yet. Get the trust layer credible, open the faucet on one platform, read the real numbers, then buy compounding with data instead of hope. The tiers below stack in exactly that order, and everything is month to month unless it says otherwise.
Flat Fee, Not A Percentage Of Your Spend
Ads management is $5,000/mo flat and it never grows when your budget does. A 15% agency has to see you spending $34,000/mo before they cost you less, and under roughly $17,000/mo they fall below their own minimum retainer anyway. Percentage models charge you more for scaling. Flat gets cheaper every time you turn the budget up.
$5K spend 100% $20K spend 25% $60K spend 8% Our fee as a percentage of your spend. Math, not a promise.
