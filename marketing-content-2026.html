Attribution: what it is and why it decides everything
Plain EnglishAttribution means knowing which specific ad, post or email produced a specific patient. Not a guess and not a vibe. A traceable line from the click all the way to the checkout, so you can put more money into what worked and stop paying for what did not.
Somebody clicksyour ad, post or email→Lands on your sitetagged, so we know the source→Starts the formpixel fires: form started→Submits itpixel fires: lead→Books a callpixel fires: booking→Becomes a patientpixel fires: purchase
So when you ask do we know if somebody clicks, and do we know if somebody converts, the answer is yes at every step above. Here is the plumbing that does it, and you own all three pieces.
Every click, taggedEach ad, post and email carries its own tracking tag, so a visit traces back to the exact creative that produced it. The tags live on your links.
Every conversion, firedA pixel on your site records the real actions: form started, form submitted, call booked, checkout completed. You own the pixel.
Every month, reportedThe numbers by platform and by creative, sitting next to the spend that produced them. You own the ad account and the audiences it builds.

Platform Risk, Said Up Front
We follow every community guideline and every posting precaution the platforms publish, on every account we touch. And the platforms still do what the platforms do. Accounts get restricted and accounts get banned, sometimes with no warning and no reason given. So here are the two things to know before you sign rather than after.
We cannot be responsible for a platform's decision about your account
Nobody can. Meta, TikTok, Google and the rest answer to their own enforcement systems, and any agency that promises you otherwise is selling you something they do not control.
If an account goes down mid-month, that month is still billable
Our team already produced that month's content, delivered it to your Drive, and published what you approved. The work happened, so the month is owed. This is not a policy we hide behind, it is the reason the policy exists.
If that is not a risk you want to carry, we would far rather you know it today than find out in month three. It is written the same way in your agreement, so nothing here is a surprise later.
